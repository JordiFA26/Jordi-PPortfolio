About me

Curious by nature, creative by choice.

Curiosity has always been the driving force behind everything I do, an ongoing desire to learn, explore, and understand how things work. That mindset has shaped my path into studying Automotive and Industrial Engineering at Universidad Francisco de Vitoria in Madrid, as well as pursuing challenges beyond the classroom. From completing the IB Diploma with Higher Level Physics and Chemistry to learning five languages and launching a motorsport podcast where I speak with people inside the industry, I've consistently sought out opportunities to expand my perspective.

Having lived in three countries early in life and grown up between Spanish and Mexican cultures, I've developed a broad perspective that shapes how I approach ideas and challenges. Being surrounded by people from a wide range of professional backgrounds has also exposed me to different ways of thinking and problem-solving from an early age.

Beyond academics, I'm driven by the idea of creating something meaningful. Whether it's founding an AI-focused investment club, working alongside charities, or competing in football and karting, I value discipline, curiosity, and continuous improvement. These experiences have reinforced my belief in contributing to something larger than myself.

Looking ahead, my ambition is simple: to build, to innovate, and to leave a lasting impact.

Sincerely, Jordi Facha Álvarez
