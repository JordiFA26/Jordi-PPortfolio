About the show

Where motorsport meets engineering.

Inside The Machine is a podcast dedicated to pulling back the curtain on the world of motorsport. Every episode digs into the technical side of racing — the aerodynamics, the engineering decisions, and the design philosophies that separate fast cars from championship-winning ones.

Beyond the machines themselves, the show features conversations with real people from inside the industry — engineers, designers, drivers, and analysts — sharing what it actually takes to compete at the highest levels of motorsport.
