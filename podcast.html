About the show

Where motorsport meets engineering.

Inside The Machine pulls back the curtain on the world of motorsport and car culture. Every episode digs into the technical side — the aerodynamics, the engineering decisions, and the design philosophies that separate fast cars from championship-winning ones.

Beyond the machines themselves, the show features conversations with the people who live it: a three-time Carrera Panamericana winner, professional racing drivers, Ferrari collectors, automotive designers and mechanics-turned-racers — in both English and Spanish.
